# 🎬 Eco_Road: 90-Second Hackathon Demo Video Script & Storyboard
*Prepared by Member 3 (Health Impact & Pitch Lead)*

> **Target Video Length:** 90 Seconds  
> **Target Format:** 1080p 60fps Screen Recording + Clear Voiceover  
> **Tools Recommended:** OBS Studio / Loom / CleanShot X + Browser in Fullscreen (F11)  
> **URL to Record:** `http://localhost:3000`

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Storyboard Matrix

| Time | Screen Action / Visual Focus | Voiceover Narration (Spoken Text) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 – 0:15**<br/>*(The Hook)* | • Browser opens to `http://localhost:3000`<br/>• Mouse hovers over the clean Google Maps interface and ambient AQI pill.<br/>• Zoom into campus map showing green trails. | *"Meet Alex, a university student walking across campus. Like millions of students, Alex takes the fastest road recommended by standard navigation apps—unaware that walking past the perimeter bus terminal exposes their lungs to the equivalent of half a cigarette every 20 minutes. Current maps prioritize vehicle speed; they are completely blind to air toxicity."* |
| **0:15 – 0:35**<br/>*(The Dual Route)* | • Click 1-Click Preset: **`🏫 Hostel ➔ Central Library`**<br/>• Dual routes instantly render: Blue line (Shortest) & Green glowing line (Eco).<br/>• Click on **`🟢 Clean Air (Eco)`** tab. | *"We built Eco_Road: clean-air walking navigation designed for campus pedestrians. With one click, Eco_Road calculates two paths: the shortest route alongside heavy diesel traffic, and the Clean Air Corridor through our campus botanical arboretum. Our algorithm scores street-level AQI using open environmental sensors."* |
| **0:35 – 0:50**<br/>*(Health Impact & Forecast)* | • Hover over the **Respiratory Exposure Savings** banner.<br/>• Click **`📊 Compare Health Impact & Grades`**.<br/>• Modal opens showing: **Grade C (Unhealthy)** vs. **Grade A+ (Pristine)**, PM2.5 Inhaled (µg), and cigarettes avoided.<br/>• Close modal; point to **6-Hour Forecast Timeline**. | *"Instead of abstract AQI numbers, Eco_Road calculates exact biological inhalation: taking the Eco Corridor saves 38.4 micrograms of PM2.5—a 71% reduction in toxic exposure, saving Alex from the particulate damage of 1.5 cigarettes today for just 2 extra minutes of walking. Our 6-hour forecast warns students of upcoming rush hour surges."* |
| **0:50 – 1:15**<br/>*(Live Turn Navigation & Walk Simulator)* | • Click **`🎮 Simulate Walk (2x)`**.<br/>• Camera auto-centers and zooms in (Google Maps HUD appears).<br/>• Marker moves with rotating heading cone.<br/>• Top green banner counts down: *In 80 m, Turn Left*.<br/>• Turn chime plays and voice announces turn. | *"When Alex starts walking, Eco_Road launches a full Google Maps turn-by-turn navigation experience. Our built-in Walk Simulator lets judges experience real-time heading rotation, dynamic auto-re-centering, synthesized turn chimes, and Web Speech voice guidance indoors without losing GPS signal."* |
| **1:15 – 1:25**<br/>*(Hotspot Shield & Detour)* | • Marker approaches the red **Main Transit Bay** circle.<br/>• Red banner pops up: *⚠️ Hotspot (Transit Bay): AQI 260*.<br/>• Warning chime plays.<br/>• Click **`🌿 Avoid Hotspot`** button.<br/>• Route instantly bends onto shaded green trail. | *"When a localized pollution spike or idling diesel bus is detected ahead, Eco_Road's Hotspot Shield alerts the student and calculates an instant clean detour around the danger zone."* |
| **1:25 – 1:30**<br/>*(Closing Vision)* | • Marker arrives at destination: celebratory chime rings.<br/>• Pan to show the open-source tech stack: Leaflet, OSRM, FastAPI, WAQI. | *"100% free. 100% open source. Protecting student respiratory health one step at a time. This is Eco_Road."* |
