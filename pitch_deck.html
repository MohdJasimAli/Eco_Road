📐 Scientific Methodology

Biological Respiratory Exposure Dosage

Moving beyond abstract AQI numbers into peer-reviewed inhalation toxicology.

The Dosage Formula
Inhaled PM2.5 (μg) =
∑ [ Conc_i (μg/m³) × Time_i (min) × 0.012 m³/min ]
0.012 m³/min corresponds to standard pedestrian minute volume (~12 Liters/min).
Berkeley Earth Equivalence
Avoided Cigarettes =
Saved PM2.5 (μg) / 22.0 μg
Translates mathematical savings into actionable health motivation for students.
